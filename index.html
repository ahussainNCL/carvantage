What you get

The same standard on every car, whatever it costs

Consumer Rights Act 2015

You're buying from a trader, so the law is on your side. The car must be of satisfactory quality for its age, mileage and price, as described, and fit for purpose. If a fault was there when you bought it, you can reject the car for a full refund within 30 days.

History checked

Outstanding finance, write-offs, stolen markers and mileage are checked before a car is listed. Ask and we'll send you the report.

Faults in the listing

Scuffs, dents and known issues are photographed and written down on every car page, so nothing is a surprise when you turn up.

MOT date up front

Every listing shows when the MOT runs out. Anything with under six months left gets a fresh one before you collect.

The price is the price

No admin fees, no prep charges added at the end. Part exchange welcome on anything.

Can I take a test drive?

Yes. Book a viewing, have a proper look, and take a test drive. You're welcome to bring someone who knows cars. Message us beforehand to arrange the details.

Do you take part exchange?

Yes. Tell us about your current car and the car you're interested in. We'll give you a price for yours and the amount to pay on top. Value your part exchange.

What if the MOT is nearly due?

Every listing shows the MOT date. If there's less than six months left, we'll put a fresh 12 months on before you collect.
